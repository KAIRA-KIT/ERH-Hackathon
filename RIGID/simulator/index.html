What the model assumes
  • Geometry comes straight from your STLs: link spacing, tendon hole radii (24 mm at the base, down to 7.5 mm), hole exit heights, ball sizes, joint tilt limits and the base from the TendonServoMount script.
  • Each section bends evenly: every joint in a section takes the same angle (constant curvature). Real joints share the bend less evenly, mostly because of friction.
  • Tendons are inextensible and slide through the holes without friction. Real tendons lose some force over 12 links.
  • Statics are quasi-static: gravity on every link (125 g total, split by volume), tip payload and side push. Tensions are the least-effort set that balances the joints. Whatever moment is left over must be carried by ball-socket friction, μ × compression × ball radius.
  • ST3215-HS: 1.96 N·m stall, 2.4 A stall, 8.3 kg·cm/A, 106 rpm, 6 mm drum radius. Speed drops linearly with load.
  • "3 mid + 3 tip" ends tendons 1, 3 and 5 at Link4 (the 6th body). Switch to "All 6 to tip" if that is how you thread them.
